Carthage Mohamed Ali, TN
[ Space Manifesto ]

An independent, multidisciplinary platform based in the forest of Carthage Mohamed Ali.

Conceived as a site for situated experimentation, Drawiin operates at the intersection of visual arts, environmental documentation, critical publishing, and agroecology.

Contemporary artistic practice and research must transcend institutional and academic silos to become a shared, accessible, and grounded process. We pay close attention to the languages, terminologies, and formats we use to ensure meaningful cultural accessibility for artists, researchers, neighbors, students, and local communities.

01. Public Programming

[ Spatial Occupation ]

Drawiin’s public programming reimagines formats of engagement between contemporary art and the public sphere through participatory, experimental, and social formats.

Social & Community Formats

  • Soap Workshops: Hands-on soap-making sessions serving as a pedagogical entry point into Medicinal and Aromatic Plants (MAPs) and traditional extraction techniques.
  • Chess Club: Gatherings and tournaments promoting slow, strategic, and informal occupation of the space by artists and neighbors alike.
  • Jazz & Sound Sessions: Intimate listening sessions, sound performances, and jam sessions focused on sonic improvisation amidst the forest landscape.

Curated Screenings

  • Screening x Efest: Carte blanche dedicated to digital arts, electronic music, and moving images.
  • Screening x Salt: Documentary essays and video art exploring maritime, island, and territorial geopolitics.
  • Screening x Esseda: Screenings showcasing emerging visual narratives across North Africa and the Middle East.
  • In-Situ Outdoor Screenings (Hors-les-murs): Projections on a decommissioned transport truck in the forest clearing (featuring films by Jumana Manna and discussions with Ecologia Podcast on bioregionalism).

03. Permaculture & Situated Ecologies

[ Land & Community ]

This department challenges passive notions of "greenery" and green gentrification, framing ecological issues as inseparable from class struggle, land rights, and social justice.

Certified Training & Theory

PDC (Permaculture Design Course): Certified design courses led by Maha Mouelhi (Zerri3a.tn). The curriculum integrates soil health, water management, seed saving, and natural building within the context of Carthage Mohamed Ali.

Neighborhood Engagement

  • Communal Garden & Seed Library: A collective farming space for preserving heirloom seeds and promoting community stewardship of the forest.
  • Agroecology Pop-up Market: A weekly market in partnership with Zerri3a.tn connecting local residents with organic growers and artisanal producers.
  • Workshops x Voisins (Neighbors): Co-designing landscape interventions with neighborhood youth and families to counter green gentrification.
